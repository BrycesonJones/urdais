# UCPI-RTX-5090-LISTED Child Specification

**Status: proposed child specification, version 0.1.0-draft. Not launched.** Prepared 14 September 2026. A child of the [UCPI-LISTED-GPU family specification](/docs/methodology/ucpi-listed-gpu) under the [Urdais Compute Price Index family](/docs/methodology/ucpi); everything not stated here is inherited from those documents unchanged. A different economic object from any accessible-offer child.

## Instrument

NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5090, a consumer card on a PCIe board with 32 GB GDDR7, rented as a whole device in the per-accelerator allocation class. Virtualized slices, shared hosts, consumer marketplace hosts observed as a platform aggregate, and serverless inference products are excluded by the family's full-device, service-product and marketplace rules.

## Admitted upstream SKUs

Price of Compute canonical SKU `RTX-5090`. Admitted as Grade C identity.

## Seller topology evidence, 14 September 2026

Runpod: RTX 5090 32 GB listed per GPU with per-GPU host resources (Secure Cloud), a dedicated full-device rental. Vast.ai: platform aggregate across independent hosts, excluded. No other seller appeared in the source on first retrieval.

## Status on first retrieval

One eligible seller. Under the family's structural rule the instrument is **Unavailable** (`SINGLE_PARTICIPANT`) and no candidate value exists. This is a valid result; the instrument stays defined so that a second independent legal seller makes it calculable without new methodology.
