## The collision with UMPI V1

UMPI-DRAM Spot V1, as frozen by the approved-in-review Phase 2A methodology, **publishes the selected source close unchanged** as the displayed price in USD per chip. It applies no arithmetic to the price. The Urdais 1D close-to-close change is a genuine calculation, but it sits beside the price rather than replacing it.

The only route TrendForce named is publication "**only after a complete re-analysis**". Publishing the close unchanged is the opposite of that. So:

> **The UMPI V1 product as defined is outside the one licensing door TrendForce opened, on the plain reading of this reply.**

This is not a rights gate Urdais can close by asking the same question again. Closing it requires either a licence that explicitly permits public commercial display of the raw price values — which the reply does not offer and the public terms forbid — or a change to what UMPI V1 publishes, which is a product decision under a new methodology version and not a Phase 2B decision.

## Public terms, re-verified 22 September 2026

Retrieved from `https://www.dramexchange.com/About/TermsOfUse`, last updated 1 January 2020. Unchanged from the Phase 1B reading.

**§6.2** — the bar that covers both raw republication and derived works:

> YOU MAY NOT REPRODUCE, MODIFY, CREATE DERIVATIVE WORKS FROM, DISPLAY, PERFORM, PUBLISH, DISTRIBUTE, DISSEMINATE, BROADCAST OR CIRCULATE TO ANY THIRD PARTY

**§6.3** — the attribution form, which applies only once a use is authorized:

> USERS ARE REQUIRED TO INCLUDE A NOTICE INDICATING THAT THE WEBSITE IS THE SOURCE OF THE MATERIAL

**§5.h** — the speculative-use clause:

> YOU ARE PROHIBITED FROM USING, REFERENCING, INCORPORATING, OR RELYING UPON ANY PRICES, DATA, FORECASTS, RESEARCH, OR OTHER CONTENT PROVIDED THROUGH THE WEBSITE IN CONNECTION WITH ANY BETTING, WAGERING, PREDICTION MARKET, OR OTHER SPECULATIVE ACTIVITY

Three observations on §5.h, and no legal conclusion beyond the text. The clause governs **Urdais's own use**, and a published informational benchmark is not on its face a bet. "OR OTHER SPECULATIVE ACTIVITY" is open-ended and undefined in the text read. "REFERENCING" is broad, but a third party referencing a published UMPI value is a different actor performing a different act, and this clause binds the user of the website. The wording is broad enough that a written carve-out is prudent rather than optional; it is not a basis for concluding that a market-data benchmark is prohibited.

The terms document contains **no clause on automated access, robots or data mining**, and **no clause on retention or deletion of materials after termination**. Under the repository's ambiguity rule, silence is neither permission nor prohibition: post-termination retention is `ambiguous_requires_written_confirmation`, not permitted.
