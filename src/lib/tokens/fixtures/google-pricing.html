Batch

Batch is published at half the standard rate for the rows above. Batch is a separate service tier and is not part of the standardized workload.

Context caching

Context caching is published separately per model, plus a storage price per 1,000,000 tokens per hour. Storage is charged per hour rather than per token and is out of the per-1M-token unit.
