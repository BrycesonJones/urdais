Models & Pricing

The prices listed below are in units of per 1M tokens. Billing is based on the total number of input and output tokens.

Off-peak rates are half of the peak rates. Peak hours are 01:00 - 04:00 and 06:00 - 10:00 UTC, Monday through Friday (all other hours are off-peak).

There is no standard, peak-neutral rate on this table. Every published price is a peak rate or an off-peak rate.
